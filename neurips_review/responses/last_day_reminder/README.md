## Posting rules

- One comment per reviewer, posted **inside that reviewer's own thread** (reply to
  our v12 rebuttal comment there). Each reply notifies that reviewer and the AC.
- Do **not** post a single global comment addressed to all reviewers. Lower
  salience, and it visibly reads as a form letter.
- Leave visibility at the default (Reviewers + AC + Authors). The AC is
  arbitrating a 2/4/5 split and should see the follow-up.
- Post each once. Do not follow up on the follow-up.

## Drafting decisions

- **No exact time remaining.** "The author discussion window is drawing to a
  close, and it is our last opportunity to respond" carries the urgency, cannot
  be factually wrong about the deadline, and frames the deadline as information
  for the reviewer rather than pressure. Reviewers and the AC keep discussing
  after this window closes; authors cannot.
- **One sentence of reviewer-specific content in each.** Not decoration. The AC
  reads these comments while forming a recommendation and has said the decision
  turns on whether structural gains translate to executable simulations, so the
  execution numbers should appear one more time. It also lets a reviewer act
  without re-reading a long rebuttal, and for kEdh it is the only thing that
  answers a "hard to understand" objection.
- **No request to raise a score anywhere**, including gep1, who wrote explicit
  conditional score commitments. The numbers make the mapping obvious; restating
  the commitment back at them would read as pressure.
- **No salutation.** The v12 responses open with a heading and go straight into
  "We thank the reviewer...", so these match that register. A "Dear Reviewer X,"
  under those comments reads more formal, and more like a mail-merge, than what
  came before it.
- No em dashes, per author preference.
